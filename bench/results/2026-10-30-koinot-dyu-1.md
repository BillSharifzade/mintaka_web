# Benchmark run · 2026-10-30-koinot-dyu-1

Written by `bench/run.sh`; method and how to read it in `bench/README.md`. The load generator ran
on the **same machine** as the server, so both compete for the same cores.

This is the production host (Koinot Innovations and Technologies, Dushanbe): a 2-vCPU virtual
machine shared with other services. The run was started with `nice -n 10`, at modest rates, to
leave them undisturbed (load average 0.04 before, 0.20 after); it measures the server, not what
that machine could do flat out. Command:
`bench/run.sh --label koinot-dyu-1 --server ~/mintaka-prod/bin/mintaka-prod --dist ~/mintaka-prod/dist --rates "1000 4000" --seconds 10 --conns 32`.
The `aborted due to deadline` errors are requests still in flight when oha's 10 s ended.
